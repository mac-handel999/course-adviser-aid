Advyza — User Guide

Creating an account

Sign in with your institutional email address. If you do not have an account yet, click “Create one” on the sign-in page and enter your email, a password, and your full name. Your full name is stored in your profile and is used to generate your initials avatar. If you created an account before the full-name field existed, the avatar will fall back to the first letter of your email.

Setting up your department

In the Settings view, fill in:

  • Faculty / School — e.g. “School of Health Technology (SOHT)”
  • Department — e.g. “Department of Public Health”
  • Portal slug — a short, unique identifier (lowercase letters, numbers, and hyphens only) that becomes the public URL students use to check their results.
  • Class Set — the admission cohort session (e.g. “2024/2025”). This auto-populates academic session labels for each year.

These details appear on every official document’s letterhead — exported Excel files, printed PDFs, transcripts, and the student portal.

Configuring credit load

In the Settings view, under “Credit load,” set the expected total credit units for each year and semester (e.g. Year 1 Harmattan: 24 units). The Cumulative Result Sheet will then show each student’s completion percentage next to their GPA.

Launching a course and entering results

Each semester shows a list of launched courses. Click “Launch Course”, enter the course code, title, and credit unit, then add students one by one with “+ Add student row” or import from file (CSV/XLSX). Scores are graded automatically (5-point scale) as you type.

When you are inside a course roster, only rows belonging to that course are shown. Imported rows are automatically linked to the open course.

Cumulative Result Sheet

Select any year and semester in the Cumulative view to see a table of all students and all courses side by side, with grades, points, and pass/fail remarks. Click Export to Excel or Print / PDF.

Generating transcripts

In the Transcript Generator, enter a student’s registration number and click Generate. The full academic record across all years and semesters is compiled, including semester GPAs and the cumulative CGPA. You can then print or export to Excel.

Sharing the Student Results Portal

Your portal slug (set in Settings) creates a public URL: https://[your-domain]/students-results/[slug]. Students visit this link, enter the passcode you set, then enter their registration number to view their results. From the Profile page you can copy the link or visit the portal directly.

Passcodes: The passphrase is bcrypt-hashed and never stored in plaintext. It cannot be displayed or recovered. If forgotten, set a new one — this immediately replaces the old one for all students.

Exporting to Excel and printing to PDF

Every sheet — per-course roster, whole-semester/year export, Cumulative Result Sheet, and Transcript Generator — can be exported to a properly formatted Excel file with centered cells, content-based column widths, and a letterhead that includes the academic session. Use Print / PDF to save a paper-ready PDF; all buttons and navigation are hidden automatically in the printed output.
