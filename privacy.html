Privacy & Data Policy

Important: This is a starting template and has not been reviewed by a lawyer. Review with qualified legal counsel before relying on it. Because this app handles student academic records, you should specifically consider compliance with Nigeria’s Data Protection Act (NDPA 2019) and any other applicable data-protection regulations.

How data is collected

  • Adviser accounts: Email and password are created via Supabase Auth. You may optionally store your full name in your profile.
  • Student results: Names, registration numbers, course codes, scores, and grades are entered by the adviser, not submitted by students directly. You are responsible for ensuring you have appropriate authority to record each student’s data.
  • Passcodes: Student passcodes are bcrypt-hashed and never stored in plaintext. The plaintext is permanently unrecoverable.
  • Department settings: Faculty, department name, portal slug, class set, credit load targets, and academic session labels are stored per adviser account.

How data is stored

All adviser accounts and result data are hosted on Supabase (PostgreSQL database) with per-user Row-Level Security. The application backend runs on Vercel as serverless functions. Data is never sold, and is only accessible to the account that created it (or an authorised department administrator acting on that account).

Data processing and retention

Data is retained for as long as your adviser account remains active. Deleting your account permanently removes all associated results, courses, and settings. We do not use your data for machine learning, advertising, or profiling of any kind.

Cookies and tracking

We use essential cookies only, to keep you signed in during your browser session and to save your theme preference. No analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking scripts are loaded.

Your responsibilities

As the data controller for the student records you enter, you are responsible for:

  • Ensuring you have the authority to record each student’s academic results;
  • Using a strong, unique passcode for the student portal and sharing it only with authorised recipients;
  • Informing students that their results are available through the portal and of the security measures in place;
  • Complying with your institution’s own data-handling and retention policies, as well as applicable Nigerian data-protection laws.
