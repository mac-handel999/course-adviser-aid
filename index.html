Everything the department's result sheets used to do — in one place

The portal replaces the old spreadsheet-and-macro workflow with a system that grades, totals, and files results as they're entered.

Year-by-year sheets

Separate Harmattan and Rain semester sheets for Year 1 through Year 10, each carrying the department's letterhead.

Automatic grading

Scores are converted to grades and grade points the moment they're entered, on the standard 5-point scale.

Transcript generator

Search a registration number to compile a student's full record across every year, with semester and cumulative GPA.

Cloud-backed accounts

Sign in to keep results synced and accessible from any computer in the department office.

How it works

01

Enter results by semester

Add each student's registration number, course, credit unit, and score to the relevant year and semester sheet.

02

Grades total themselves

The portal calculates grade, grade point, and a live semester summary — total students, passes, and GPA range.

03

Generate and export

Pull a full transcript for any student, print it, or export any sheet or transcript to Excel.
